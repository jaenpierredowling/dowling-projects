WHAT WE'RE ABOUT

A team that gets the work done.

Dowling Projects helps people get projects done properly.

Sometimes that means maintaining or improving an existing property, or building something completely custom. Sometimes it also means sourcing suppliers, coordinating several contractors and managing the project from start to finish.

We work with homeowners, Airbnb owners, guest houses, businesses, property managers, farms, contractors and film productions across the Western Cape.

COMMON QUESTIONS
Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Initial phone and WhatsApp consultations are free.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

Call-outs within 15 km of our Northern Suburbs base are free. Travel outside that area is quoted based on distance.

Can I send photos on WhatsApp?

Yes. Photos and videos often help us understand the job before a site visit is arranged.

Do you charge a day rate?

No. Every project is assessed and quoted according to the work required.

Can you manage other contractors?

Yes. We can coordinate contractors, trades and suppliers where a project requires several people.

Do you work outside Cape Town?

Yes. We work throughout the Western Cape and can take on work further afield when the project warrants the travel.
